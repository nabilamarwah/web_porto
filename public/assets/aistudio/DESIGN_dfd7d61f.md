# Design System: Canva Editorial Portfolio (Nabila Marwah Az Zahra)

## 1. Visual Identity & Brand Personality
- **Aesthetic**: Bold Canva Editorial / Graphic Retro-Modern Web
- **Tone**: Youthful, energetic, friendly, confident, and editorial
- **Key Characteristics**: Warm cream background, ultra-bold black display type, road-marking dashed divider bands, electric blue doodle stickers, bright yellow accent pills, and heavy rounded card geometry.

## 2. Color Palette (Design Tokens)
- `--cream`: `#FBF8EE` (Primary background, warm flat canvas)
- `--ink`: `#0E0E0E` (Primary dark tone, display headlines, black bands, high contrast)
- `--yellow`: `#FFE81F` (Primary accent, CTA buttons, active pills, highlight cards)
- `--blue`: `#2F5BEA` (Secondary accent, hand-drawn vector doodle stickers, script accents)
- `--white`: `#FFFFFF` (Card surfaces, inverted typography, crisp container backgrounds)
- `--grey-tile`: `#C9C6BF` (Process number tiles, neutral soft cards)
- `--ink-soft`: `rgba(14, 14, 14, 0.62)` (Subtext, meta descriptions, secondary labels)
- `--line`: `#0E0E0E` (Borders, card outlines, stroke treatments)

## 3. Typography
- **Display Headlines**: `Archivo`, sans-serif
  - Weight: 900 (Black)
  - Text Transform: UPPERCASE
  - Font Stretch: 110% - 112%
  - Letter Spacing: -0.02em
  - Line Height: 0.86 - 0.95
  - Stylistic Effects: Slit-letter cut (skewed slice cut-through) and Flip-letter (180deg inverted glyphs like 'U')
- **Body & Structural Copy**: `DM Sans`, sans-serif
  - Weight: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 700 (Bold)
  - Body Text: 14px - 16px / Line Height 1.6
  - Caps Subtitle / Micro-copy: 10.5px - 11px, Weight 700, Letter Spacing 0.18em, UPPERCASE
- **Handwritten & Doodle Accents**: `Kaushan Script` / Handwriting
  - Weight: 400
  - Color: `--blue` (`#2F5BEA`)
  - Tilt: -6deg to -8deg

## 4. Spacing & Radius Tokens
- `--r-lg`: `clamp(28px, 3.6vw, 48px)` (High rounded photo frames)
- `--r-md`: `24px` - `26px` (Content cards, interactive containers)
- `--r-sm`: `14px` (Chips, small badge tags)
- `--r-full`: `999px` (Pill buttons, floating status badges)
- Section Padding: `100px 5vw` to `120px 5vw`
- Layout Container: `max-width: 1320px; margin: 0 auto;`

## 5. Signature Components & Patterns
- **Pill Badge (`.pill`)**:
  - Background: `--yellow` (`#FFE81F`), 2px solid `--ink` border, 999px radius.
  - Text: DM Sans 800, 11px-12px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.12em.
  - Hover: `translateY(-2px)` with shadow `0 3px 0 #0E0E0E`.
- **Road Marking Band (`.band`)**:
  - Background: `--ink` (`#0E0E0E`), full bleed.
  - Top & Bottom Borders: 5px dashed white line (`repeating-linear-gradient(90deg, #fff 0 30px, transparent 30px 50px)`).
  - Content: Marquee text ticker with yellow star separators (`✦`) or contrast experience cards.
- **Hand-Drawn Oval (`.oval`)**:
  - Double stroke irregular hand-drawn SVG oval wrapping short script/caps text.
  - Stroke: 2.2px `--blue`, rotated -6deg.
- **Photo Frames with Corner Stickers**:
  - Deep rounded photo container with playful floating corner badges:
    - Round 44px black button with white heart toggle.
    - Yellow rounded square with black thumb-up icon.
    - Speech bubble pill with `•••` dots.
- **Work Process / Skill Numbered Tiles**:
  - Square cards with giant numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`) in `Archivo` 900.
  - Neutral grey surface (`--grey-tile`), transitions to vibrant yellow with tilt on hover.
